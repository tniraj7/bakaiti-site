Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Summary

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We run no servers for the app and keep no personal data about you.
  • Your stickers, packs, memes, photos you use for memes, and recent and favourite stickers stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data such as your advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Your consent choices apply.
  • Bakaiti is for people aged 13 and over.

Information we do not collect

Bakaiti does not ask you to create an account or sign in. It does not include any analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We run no servers for the app, so we do not receive, store or have access to the stickers, packs, memes or photos you use in it.

Data stored on your device

The sticker packs that come with the app ship inside it. Everything you create or choose is stored only on your device:

  • stickers, packs and memes you create;
  • photos you pick from your library to make a meme. They are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers;
  • your recent and favourite stickers.

We cannot see or recover this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads and Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use your device advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and data about how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob and the ads data

Consent and your choices

Whether you see personalised or non-personalised ads depends on your consent. Where required, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form.

You can also reset your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your device settings. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove the data stored on your device by deleting the app. If you have questions, contact us at nirajtiwari1591@gmail.com.

Sharing to other apps

When you share a sticker, the app hands it to WhatsApp or the system share sheet you choose. Those apps have their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they do with what you send.

Children

Bakaiti is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to nirajtiwari1591@gmail.com or see the contact page.
