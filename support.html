Support

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I add a pack to WhatsApp?

Open the pack in Bakaiti and tap the WhatsApp button. WhatsApp opens and asks you to confirm adding the pack. Tap Add, then find the stickers in the sticker tray of any chat. This works the same on iPhone (iOS) and Android. WhatsApp must be installed.

Stickers don’t show up in WhatsApp
  • Make sure WhatsApp is up to date and fully closed, then open it again.
  • In a chat, tap the emoji icon, then the sticker tab, and look for the pack in your list.
  • Go back to Bakaiti and add the pack again.
  • Restart your phone if the pack still does not appear.

Still nothing? Contact us with your phone model and OS version.

How do I make a meme?
  1. Tap Create meme.
  2. Pick a background colour or choose a photo from your library.
  3. Add text and emoji, and move or resize them.
  4. Save it as a sticker, or share it.
How do I create my own pack?

Tap New pack, give it a name, and add the stickers or memes you made. You can then add your pack to WhatsApp like any other pack.

Where are my stickers stored?

On your device only. We run no servers for the app, so your stickers, packs, memes and photos are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy.

Why do I see ads?

Ads help keep Bakaiti free. They are shown through Google AdMob. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change ad consent or reset my advertising ID?

Ad personalisation and your advertising ID are controlled in your device settings. On iPhone, look under Privacy & Security (Tracking and Apple Advertising). On Android, look under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID. Where consent is required, such as in the EEA and UK, you choose in the consent form the app shows.

How do I report a problem or request a sticker pack?

Use the contact page and tell us what happened, or which pack you would like to see. To report content, see Copyright and content reports.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Android 8.0 (API level 26) or later.
