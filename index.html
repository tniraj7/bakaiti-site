What Bakaiti does

Bakaiti is a sticker app for the way you actually chat. Pick a pack from the catalog, add it to WhatsApp in a tap, and send the right sticker at the right moment. Want something personal? Make a meme from a sticker or one of your own photos, add text and emoji, and save it as a sticker.

Text works in English, Hinglish and Hindi, and search understands how people really type, so a search like “kya kar raha hai” finds what you mean.

Made for chatting

Write memes in Hindi or Hinglish and search the way you talk, like “kya kar raha hai”.
