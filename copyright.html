Copyright and content reports

We respect the rights of creators. If you believe a sticker or other content in Bakaiti infringes your copyright, or is harmful, tell us.

What to include

  • Identification of the work you say is infringed, or a description of the harm.
  • Where the content appears in the app: the pack name and the sticker.
  • Your contact details: name, email and, if you wish, address.
  • A statement that you act in good faith and that the information you give is accurate.

Where to send it

Email your report to nirajtiwari1591@gmail.com. We will review it and remove the content where appropriate.
